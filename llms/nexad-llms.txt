# Nexad

> Nexad (About Intelligence, Inc.) is an AI-native advertising company. It ships the Nexad Android Ads SDK for embedding contextual, native, WebView, chat-message, and modal-overlay ads into mobile and AI-chat apps, and operates Soku AI — an autonomous marketing agent (the NexStudio API) that runs ads across Google, Meta, TikTok, and ChatGPT Ads with a 24/7 perceive–decide–act loop and human-in-the-loop approvals. Generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the catalog; the provider does not publish an llms.txt.

## Products
- [Nexad Android Ads SDK](https://docs.nex.ad/docs/android/intro): Android SDK (Java/Kotlin) for native, WebView, chat-message, and modal ad formats.
- [Soku AI](https://soku.ai): Autonomous AI marketing agent; NexStudio API at https://api.soku.ai.

## What Soku AI does
Soku AI is an agentic, chat-driven marketing tool that:
- Generates image and video ad creative from a prompt or a product URL
- Launches live campaigns natively across Meta, Google, and TikTok (no manual export-and-rebuild)
- Optimizes spend and bids continuously toward a ROAS or CAC target
- A/B-tests creatives on live traffic and scales the winners
- Reads GA4 and ad-account analytics to ground its decisions
- Researches competitor ads to inform creative angles and positioning

## Free tools (citable definitions)
- ROAS calculator (https://soku.ai/tools/roas-calculator): ROAS = revenue from ads ÷ ad spend, as a multiple. Measures revenue, not profit.
- Break-even ROAS calculator (https://soku.ai/tools/break-even-roas-calculator): Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ gross margin. At 40% margin, break-even ROAS = 2.5×.
- CAC calculator (https://soku.ai/tools/cac-calculator): CAC = acquisition spend ÷ new customers acquired. Blended CAC can be lower than paid CAC when organic acquisition contributes, depending on the cost definition.
- LTV:CAC calculator (https://soku.ai/tools/ltv-cac-calculator): Ratio = LTV ÷ CAC; 3× or higher is the common benchmark for healthy unit economics.
- Blended MER calculator (https://soku.ai/tools/blended-mer-calculator): MER = total revenue ÷ total marketing spend across all channels.
- Ad budget calculator (https://soku.ai/tools/ad-budget-calculator): Budget = target customers × CAC, or target revenue ÷ target ROAS.

## How Soku compares
- vs AdCreative.ai (https://soku.ai/alternatives/adcreative-ai): AdCreative.ai generates creative and stops; Soku also launches and optimizes campaigns, with native TikTok deployment.
- vs Madgicx (https://soku.ai/alternatives/madgicx): Madgicx is a deep Meta-focused dashboard; Soku spans creative, multi-channel launch (Meta, Google, TikTok), and optimization in one chat agent.
